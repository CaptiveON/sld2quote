SPECIFICATION EXTRACT, SECTION 7: LV SWITCHBOARD (sheet-1007)

7.1 The switchboard shall be designed, manufactured and verified in accordance with BS EN 61439-2.
7.2 Internal separation shall be Form 4 Type 2 as defined in the UK National Annex.
7.3 The assembly shall withstand a prospective short-circuit current of 36 kA for 1 second at the main busbars.
7.4 Outgoing moulded-case circuit breakers shall be 4-pole with adjustable thermal-magnetic or electronic trip units.
7.5 Degree of protection shall be not less than IP31 with doors closed.
7.6 Main busbars shall be hard-drawn high-conductivity copper, tin-plated at joints.
7.7 The contractor shall provide type-test or design-verification certificates prior to manufacture.
7.8 Two years' spares for all trip units shall be included.
