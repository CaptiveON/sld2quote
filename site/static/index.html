sld2quote: a miniature of the KTP system, measured

I built a small, measured version of the system this KTP sets out to build: a switchboard drawing and its specification in, a priced bill of materials with the evidence for every line out.

This site is written for the assessor at the University of Salford and for R&B Switchgear. It shows the pipeline reading a drawing you choose, the numbers it was scored on, the decisions behind those numbers, the problems met on the way, and how each part would be rebuilt at full scale inside the partnership.

In plain language

What this is, and what it is not

An estimator at a switchgear maker receives a single-line diagram and a specification and turns them into a priced bill of materials. That takes hours of reading, counting and checking, and a missed clause or a misread rating costs money. This miniature does that reading on synthetic drawings and shows its evidence, so the gain and the gaps can be measured before any real drawing is involved.

What it is

  • A working pipeline: text reading, symbol detection, label linking, a specification check, rule-based pricing, and a quotation with the evidence for every line.
  • Measured end to end: a generator draws the drawings and writes their answer keys, so every stage has a score, and every score on this site is read from the evaluation files at load time.
  • Live: the server reads the drawing you choose while you watch, stage by stage, and the result opens with the drawing, the parts and the questions an estimator must still decide.
  • Honest about its limits: the symbol detector was trained on synthetic drawings; the link rules were tuned on the sets they are scored on; the real public-domain drawings are shown unscored.

What it is not

  • Not R&B's system, and not built on any R&B drawing, part or price. The catalogue is invented and labelled as such.
  • Not a claim that real consultants' drawings would score like these. They would score lower, and the first weeks of the KTP are labelling real drawings to measure that gap and close it.
  • Not a language model guessing a quotation. A rules layer owns the ratings, the forms and the parts; nothing doubtful is settled silently.
  • Not finished. It is a partial solution built in a week to show the shape of the full one, with the road map for the rest.
