  2. Bhanbhro, H., Hooi, Y. K., Kusakunniran, W., Zakaria, M. N. B., Hashmi, S. A. M., Amur, Z. H. and Memon, V. (2026).   3. Stürmer, J. M., Graumann, M. and Koch, T. (2024). From Engineering Diagrams to Graphs: Digitizing P&IDs with Transformers. arXiv:2411.13929; published at the IEEE International Conference on Data Science and Advanced Analytics (DSAA) 2025. arxiv.org/abs/2411.13929As used here: symbols and their connections extracted together as a graph, the direction for the learned link after the KTP (architecture; problem 12).
  4. Prasad, S. and Mahapatra, P. (2026). SynthPID: P&ID digitization from Topology-Preserving Synthetic Data. arXiv:2604.16513. arxiv.org/abs/2604.16513As used here: 665 synthetic P&IDs that keep real topology trained a model to within 8 points of a model trained on real drawings; the case for synthetic data as a start, and for seeding the generator with real topologies (decision 1; problem 1; architecture; KTP risks).

The detector
