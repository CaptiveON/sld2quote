The evidence

Every number, with its plain reading

This page is the measurement record. Each chart and table is drawn from the evaluation files the code wrote (the scorecards, the detector summary, the text-reader comparison and the chart data), never typed in, and each comes with a sentence saying what it means. Synthetic drawings only; the real public-domain drawings in the gallery are not scored, because they have no answer key yet.

How to read it: the test set is drawings in the styles the detector trained on, never seen in training; the unseen-style set holds whole drawing styles out of training; the baseline set is the drawings of the first measurement. Each comes as a clean copy and a scan-like copy. "Trained" is the site's reader; "classical" is the hand-written shape-test reader it replaced, kept as the before.

In charts

The evidence, drawn

Nine charts from the evaluation files; "Show the numbers" under each gives the same data as a table. The plain reading, chart by chart: the trained reader finds nearly every symbol where the classical one found few; the rare device types are the hard ones; weight averaging made the training curves steady; the threshold was chosen where boxes on blank paper stop; the confidence shown is how often a box with that score was right; the chosen text reader is both accurate and runs on Linux; the time goes to reading, not to rules; the training data is long-tailed; the price error is small on most drawings with a short tail.

Whole drawings

The scorecards

Whole drawings, scored against their answer keys: the trained reader against the hand-written one it replaced, on each evaluation set. Symbols found is the share of the answer key's symbols the reader found with the right type; price within is the gap between the quoted total and the total the same rules give on the answer key, so it measures reading, not pricing.

Labels linked right is counted between devices that were found and texts that were read, so it measures the pairing alone; a correct pairing can still hold a misread rating. The link rules were written against this generator's conventions and tuned on the sets they are scored on, so it is an upper bound.

Plain reading: on the test set the trained reader finds of the symbols on clean copies where the classical reader found , and its quoted total lands within of the answer key's. The detector on its own

Training runs, per tile

Per 640 px tile on the unseen-style test tiles, with duplicate suppression; the size rule asks for precision and recall of at least 0.95 for every class on validation and unseen-style tiles. The live model's row is its deployed pass, chosen on whole drawings.

What it still gets wrong

Plain reading: every run is recorded, including the ones that lost. Per-tile scores are a floor for the product, because a symbol cut by a tile edge is whole in the next tile, and they can disagree with the drawing-level score, which is why the live pass was chosen on whole drawings: , pass , mAP@0.5 on unseen-style tiles; classes meet the 0.95 bar, which the full system does not yet, and says so.
